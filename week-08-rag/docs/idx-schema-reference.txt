RETS_PROPERTY SCHEMA REFERENCE

L_ListingID:
MLS system listing ID.

L_Address:
Full street address.

L_City:
City of the property.

L_SystemPrice:
Current listing price.

L_Keyword2:
Number of bedrooms.

LM_Dec_3:
Number of bathrooms.

LM_Int2_3:
Approximate finished square footage.

L_Type_:
Property subtype, such as SingleFamilyResidence or Condominium.

L_Status:
Current listing status, such as Active, Pending, or Withdrawn.

L_Remarks:
Full listing description written by the listing agent.

YearBuilt:
Year the property was constructed.

AssociationFee:
HOA or association fee.

PoolPrivateYN:
Whether the property has a private pool.

DaysOnMarket:
Number of days the property has been on the market.

CALIFORNIA_SOLD SCHEMA REFERENCE

The california_sold table contains historical closed real estate transactions.

Important california_sold columns:

City:
City where the sold property is located.

ClosePrice:
Final sale price of the property.

CloseDate:
Date the real estate transaction officially closed.

ListPrice:
Listing price used for comparison with the final sale price.

OriginalListPrice:
Original asking price when the property was first listed.

LivingArea:
Interior living area or square footage of the property.

DaysOnMarket:
Number of days the property was on the market.

PropertyType:
Type or category of the property.

YearBuilt:
Year the property was constructed.

BedroomsTotal:
Number of bedrooms.

BathroomsTotalInteger:
Number of bathrooms.

StandardStatus:
Standardized MLS status of the property.

The california_sold table is used for historical market analysis, including average sale prices, price per square foot, days on market, sales volume, price trends, and list-to-close ratios.

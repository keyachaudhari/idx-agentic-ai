TRESTLE PROPERTY METADATA - SELECTED FIELD DEFINITIONS

ListingKey:
Primary key that uniquely identifies a record in the Trestle Property resource.

ListingId:
Listing identifier from the MLS system.

StandardStatus:
Standardized listing status used to identify the current lifecycle state of a property.

PostalCode:
Postal or ZIP code associated with the property.

AssociationFee:
Fee paid by the homeowner to a Home Owners Association for maintenance of common areas, neighborhood services, or association-related benefits.

AssociationFeeFrequency:
Frequency at which an association fee is paid, such as weekly, monthly, or annually.

AssociationName:
Name of the Home Owners Association.

AssociationYN:
Indicates whether the property is part of a Home Owners Association.

BathroomsFull:
Number of full bathrooms.

BathroomsHalf:
Number of half bathrooms.

BathroomsTotalInteger:
The simple total number of bathrooms.

BedroomsTotal:
Total number of bedrooms in the dwelling.

BuildingAreaTotal:
Total area of the structure, including finished and unfinished areas.

AboveGradeFinishedArea:
Finished area within the structure at or above ground level.

BelowGradeFinishedArea:
Finished area within the structure below ground level.

BuyerAgentFirstName:
First name of the buyer's agent.

BuyerAgentLastName:
Last name of the buyer's agent.

BuyerAgentFullName:
Full name of the buyer's agent.

BuyerOfficeName:
Legal name of the brokerage representing the buyer.

BackOnMarketDate:
Date when a listing that had gone off market returned to the market.

AccessibilityFeatures:
List or description of accessibility features included with the property.

Appliances:
List of appliances included in the sale or lease.

ArchitecturalStyle:
Style of the property structure, such as Victorian, Ranch, or Craftsman.

AssociationAmenities:
Amenities provided by the Home Owners Association or community.
